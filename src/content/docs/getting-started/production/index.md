## Production deployments

Use this path when you are planning or performing a real UDS Core deployment on a managed Kubernetes cluster.

This path focuses on three things:

- **Production prerequisites** – what needs to be in place before you deploy.
- **Build your bundle** – capturing your target environment and mission needs.
- **Deploy UDS Core** – using your bundle to deploy to your cluster.
